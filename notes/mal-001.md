# 玛拉基书 第1章

## 本章

1耶和华的话，藉玛拉基传给以色列的默示。
**耶和华爱以色列**
2耶和华说：“我曾爱你们。”你们却说：“你在何事上爱我们呢？”耶和华说：“以扫不是雅各的哥哥吗？我却爱雅各， 3恶以扫，使他的山岭荒凉，把他的地业交给旷野的野狗。” 4以东若说：“我们虽被毁坏，却要重建荒废之处。”万军之耶和华如此说：“任他们建造，我必拆毁；人必称他们为‘邪恶之境’，为‘耶和华永远恼怒之民’。” 5你们必亲眼看见，你们要说：“耶和华在以色列疆界之外必尊为大！”

《玛拉基书》1:1-5 笔记：
神爱雅各，并非因他有任何值得爱之处，乃是基于神在万世以先、在基督里的拣选。唯有神的独生爱子基督是全然可爱的。神憎恶以扫，是因祂的弃绝，亦因以扫对神的离弃；以扫并无寻求神的意志与力量。这正如我们的“旧人”：旧人不爱神，神亦憎恶旧人，其终局乃是与基督同钉十字架。
旧人虽致力于建造并成就属世的骄傲，终必被拆毁。若主耶稣不是我们的至宝，一切作为皆为草木禾秸，大至帝国或哲学体系亦然。神主权的拣选跨越古今，选民并非生于血气、情欲或人意，乃是生于神。因此，人不能凭私意使他人（包括至亲）成为选民，这唯独出于神恩典的主权。
**English Version:**
Notes on Malachi 1\:1-5\:
God’s love for Jacob was not due to any inherent loveliness in him, but was based on God’s election in Christ before the ages began. Only Christ, the beloved and only begotten Son of God, is truly lovely. God’s hatred of Esau stems from His reprobation and Esau’s own abandonment of God; Esau possessed no power to choose God. This mirrors our "old man": the old self does not love God, and God abhors the old self, whose destiny is to be crucified with Christ.
Though the old man may build extensively to achieve personal pride, it will ultimately be torn down. Unless the Lord Jesus is our supreme treasure, all our labors—be they empires or philosophical schools—are but wood, hay, and stubble. God’s sovereign election remains constant from antiquity to the present; the elect are born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God. Consequently, we cannot make anyone (even our most beloved family or friends) one of the elect through human will; it rests solely upon God’s sovereign grace and choice.

旧人在圣工及一切神所喜悦的事上一无所能，其唯一的结局乃是钉十字架。或有人争辩：难道旧人竟无半点用处或功劳吗？断乎没有。
因为圣工与神所喜悦的一切，非人手所能成就，亦非天使所能达成，唯独出于神大能的膀臂。试问，创造万有的神岂需人手（甚至天使）的服侍？神若要成就心中所喜悦的，岂需假借受造之物为不可或缺的工具？既是如此，旧人有何地位可言？神绝非如人一般诡诈虚伪——祂不会在永远憎恶某一物的同时，又偶尔觉得其尚有可利用之处。祂的嫌恶与拣选皆是绝对且圣洁的。
**English Version:**
The "old man" is utterly powerless in sacred works and in all that is pleasing to God; his sole purpose is to be crucified. One might argue: is there not a single use or merit in him? Truly, there is none.
For sacred works and all that pleases God are accomplished neither by human strength nor by angelic power, but solely by the mighty arm of God Himself. Does the Creator of all things require the service of human hands, or even of angels? When God fulfills His good pleasure, does He depend on any tool as a necessity? What part, then, does the old man play? God is not deceitful or hypocritical like man; He does not eternally abhor something while occasionally finding it useful. His holiness and sovereignty admit no such inconsistency.

**斥责祭司**
6万军之耶和华对你们说：“儿子孝敬父亲，仆人敬畏主人；我既为父亲，孝敬我的在哪里呢？我既为主人，敬畏我的在哪里呢？你们这些藐视我名的祭司啊！”你们却说：“我们在何事上藐视你的名呢？” 7“你们将不洁净的食物献在我的祭坛上，却说：‘我们在何事上使你不洁净呢？’你们说，耶和华的供桌是可藐视的。 8你们将瞎眼的献为祭物，这不算为恶吗？将瘸腿的、有病的献上，这不算为恶吗？那么，请把这些献给你的省长，他岂会悦纳你，岂会抬举你呢？这是万军之耶和华说的。”

我们已经得出结论：旧人如同被弃绝的以扫，全然无用。既然如此，旧人怎能向神献祭？即便旧人甘愿焚献自身，神岂能悦纳？神并无缺乏；他曾宣告，纵有缺乏也无需告知世人。
然而，神吩咐以色列人献祭，并分别利未人承担祭司职分。神并未向万民颁布此令，亦未在万民中设立祭司。这一吩咐仅针对神所拣选、分别出来的以色列人。
既然献祭的事宜——包括祭司职分与礼仪——均由神向其选民颁布，祭物难道能由献祭者自行决定吗？大祭司之职并非人所自选，乃是神所设立。即便大祭司，岂能随己意而行？那绝非神的意思，亦不能得神喜悦。
因为“在耶和华的山上必有预备”。神吩咐亚伯拉罕献上那凭应许所生的独生子以撒，而最终代替以撒的羊羔，正是神亲自预备的。神并未要求亚伯拉罕返回家中另选羊群，他所要的祭物，唯独是他亲自预备的那一只。
---
### English Translation
We have concluded that the "old man" is like the rejected Esau, utterly useless. If so, how can the old man offer a sacrifice to God? Even if the old man were willing to burn and offer himself, how could God accept it? God has no lack; He has declared that even if He were hungry, He would not need to tell man.
Yet, God commanded the Israelites to offer sacrifices and set apart the Levites for the priestly office. God did not issue this command to all nations, nor did He establish a priesthood among them. This was spoken solely to the Israelites, whom God had chosen and set apart.
Since the matters of sacrifice—including the priestly office and the sacrificial rites—were commanded by God to His chosen people, can the sacrifice itself be determined by the one offering it? Even the High Priest was not chosen by man but appointed by God. Can even the High Priest act according to his own will? That would never please God, nor is it His intent.
For "on the mountain of the Lord it will be provided." God commanded Abraham to offer Isaac, the only son born of the promise. The ram that ultimately replaced Isaac was provided by God Himself. God did not ask Abraham to return to his own flocks to bring an offering; the sacrifice required was the very one that God had provided.

9现在我劝你们要恳求上帝，好让他施恩给我们。这事既出于你们的手，他岂会抬举你们任何人呢？这是万军之耶和华说的。 

当我们察觉献祭不蒙悦纳、祷告未获应允，且深知自己里外全然败坏时，唯一的出路便是恳求上帝的恩典与怜悯。我们求神施恩，赐下那使人呼求的灵；否则，我们断不能得救。若在此状态下仍企图通过挣扎来实现自救，必将陷入更深的绝望，如同人在淤泥中越陷越深。
---
### English Translation
When we realize that our sacrifices are not accepted, our prayers are not answered, and that we are utterly corrupted within and without, our only recourse is to plead for God’s grace and mercy. We pray that God would graciously grant us the Spirit of grace and supplication; otherwise, we cannot be saved. To struggle in an attempt to save ourselves in such a state will only lead to deeper despair, much like a person sinking further while struggling in the mire.

《玛拉基书》1:9 笔记。
无论步履蹒跚的孩童、老人或是残障者，在坚实的地面上行走时，皆不觉有何不妥，甚至可任意奔跑跳跃。然而，若换作泥泞之地呢？纵使是体态最优美的人或健身达人，又岂能保持优雅？他们不仅无法如常，更会丑态百出，陷入窘迫。
神的恩典亦是如此。当祂赐下恩典，我们立于其上，便以为自己站立得稳。可若神撤去恩典，我们便束手无策。神难道亏欠任何人吗？断乎没有。若神亏欠人，祂所赐的便非恩典，乃是偿债。既然是恩典，神便有权决定赐予的多寡与时限。一旦祂将“坚地”变为“沼泽”——即撤去自己的恩典，人连站稳都难，遑论任意行走。
因此，我们应当放下一切巧计与谋划——你要专心依靠主，不要自以为聪明，俯伏在神面前求祂的恩典——那唯独因祂的慈爱怜悯而赐下的恩典。我们终将明白，神拆毁我们所有的计划，正是为了让我们认识祂，并在祂面前谦卑。我们会深深地厌恶自己，何等诡诈，坏到极处。如同雅各最终学会扶着杖头敬拜，并诚恳地说：“耶和华啊，我向来等候祢的救恩。”
---
### English Translation
Notes on Malachi 1:9.
Whether it is a stumbling child, the elderly, or the disabled, no one finds it unnatural to walk on firm ground; they can walk, jump, or run at will. But what if the ground turns to mire? Could even the most athletic or graceful person maintain their poise? Certainly not; they would appear clumsy and utterly distressed.
Such is the nature of God's grace. When He grants grace, we walk upon it freely, assuming we stand by our own strength. But if God were to withdraw His grace—and why shouldn't He? Does He owe anyone anything? If God owed man, then what He gives would not be grace, but the repayment of a debt. Since it is grace, He may give as much and for as long as He pleases. When the firm ground turns into a swamp, man finds it difficult even to stand, let alone move with elegance.
Therefore, let us cast aside all our clever schemes and maneuvers, and kneel before God to plead for His grace—that which is given solely out of His loving-kindness and mercy. We will eventually realize that God tears down our plans so that we may truly know Him and humble ourselves before Him. Like Jacob, who eventually worshiped while leaning on the top of his staff, saying sincerely, "I look for your deliverance, Lord."

10万军之耶和华说：“甚愿你们中间有人把殿的门关上，免得你们徒然在我坛上烧火。我不喜欢你们，也不从你们手中悦纳供物。”

《玛拉基书》1:10 交代了一个至关重要的原则：神并非被动的神，即便在人献祭的事上亦然。
神所悦纳的祭司、祭物、地点、时间与方式皆具唯一性，其余一切合宜的献祭皆是这唯一真祭——即基督受难——的预表。“这是耶和华所定的日子，我们在其中要高兴欢喜。”基督如何顺服至死，其余献祭亦当如何预表此顺服。
否则，难道人以为可以贿赂神吗？这实为人性的狂妄。人若企图以此行径越位，反倒会像该隐那样惹神愤怒；神既不喜悦该隐其人，亦不悦纳其祭物。因此，我们必须承认：除非先有祂的怜悯，否则我们本身绝无可能、亦无能力得神喜悦。我们唯有静默等候祂施恩。即便恩典尚未显明，我们也应如先知哈巴谷所言：“我只可安静等候灾难之日临到，等候那犯境之民上来。”

### English Translation
Malachi 1:10 establishes a crucial principle: God is not a passive recipient, even in the matter of man’s sacrifices.
The priest, the sacrifice, and the location, time, and manner accepted by God are unique; all other proper sacrifices are merely types and shadows of this one true sacrifice—the passion of Christ. "This is the day the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it." Just as Christ was obedient unto death, all other sacrifices must prefigure this reality.
Otherwise, does man truly believe he can bribe God? This is the height of human arrogance. Instead of pleasing Him, man provokes God’s wrath through such sacrifices, much like Cain. God neither looked with favor upon Cain nor accepted his offering. Therefore, we must confess that apart from His mercy, we are inherently unable and unwilling to please God. Our only recourse is to wait in silence for His grace. Even in its absence, we should say with the prophet Habakkuk: "I will quietly wait for the day of trouble to come upon people who invade us."

 11万军之耶和华说：“从日出之地到日落之处，我的名在列国中必尊为大。在各处，人必奉我的名烧香，献洁净的供物，因为我的名在列国中必尊为大。 12你们却亵渎我的名，说：‘主的供桌是不洁净的，供桌上的果子和食物是可藐视的。’ 13你们又说：‘看哪，这些事何等烦琐！’并嗤之以鼻。这是万军之耶和华说的。你们把抢来的、瘸腿的、有病的拿来献上为祭，我岂能从你们手中悦纳它呢？这是耶和华说的。 14行诡诈的人是可诅咒的！他的群畜中虽有公羊，他许了愿，却将有残疾的献给主。因我是大君王，我的名在列国中是可畏的。这是万军之耶和华说的。”

《玛拉基书》1:11-14 笔记。
主啊，唯有祢是真实的。祢的怜悯、慈爱、恩典、教训、受难、救恩与主权治理皆为真实。与之相对，我是虚假的，属地的一切亦如海市蜃楼，纵然气势磅礴，终究转瞬即逝。我的一切——梦想、愿望、劳碌、奔波、工作与享受——皆是虚影，因为凡虚假的必将过去，唯有真实的方能永存。
然而，在我这虚假的人里面，最真实的竟然是罪。罪所带来的咒诅与损伤是真实的，既不能免除，也无法减轻。扪心自问，我在何时、何事上没有罪呢？若使徒保罗尚且自认为罪人中的罪魁，更何况我呢？

### English Translation
Notes on Malachi 1:11-14.
Lord, You alone are true. Your mercy, loving-kindness, grace, teachings, passion, salvation, and sovereign governance are real. In contrast, I am false; everything of this earth is like a mirage—though grand in appearance, it is an illusion. All that I possess—my dreams, desires, toils, labors, pursuits, work, and pleasures—are but shadows. For all that is false shall pass away, while only what is true endures forever.
Yet, within my falsehood, the most "real" thing about me is my sin. The curse and damage brought by my sin are real; they cannot be waived, nor can they be mitigated by my own effort. In what moment or in what matter am I ever without sin? If even the Apostle Paul considered himself the chief of sinners, how much more so am I?

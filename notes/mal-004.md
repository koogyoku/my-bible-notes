# 玛拉基书 第4章

## 本章

**耶和华的日子临近**
1万军之耶和华说：“看哪，那日临近，势如烧着的火炉，凡狂傲的和行恶的都如碎秸，在那日被烧尽，根与枝条无一存留。 

玛拉基书4:1的笔记：
必有火炼的试验临到各人的工程，每个圣徒也不能避免。何况属世的一切呢？如何承受神公义审判的烈焰。若非圣灵在我们身上所结的果子，恐怕纵然明白许多的道理也是与我们无益。就像彼得跟从基督三年半，领受那么多教训，见过多少神迹，可是在使女的盘问前跌倒了。证明我们的工程无论多么大，甚至令人羡慕，可是并不能存到永远。唯有主的慈爱在我们身上，有恩典有真理，长成基督的身量才可以。其他一切净都是虚空，捕风。因为既不能经受火炼的试验，也不能长存。
**English revised version**：
Notes on Malachi 4:1:
A fiery trial will surely come upon everyone’s work; no saint can avoid it either. How much more the things of the world? How can they endure the flames of God’s righteous judgment? Unless it is the fruit produced by the Holy Spirit in us, even if we understand many doctrines, it may still be of no benefit to us. Just as Peter followed Christ for three and a half years, received so many teachings, and saw so many miracles, yet he fell before the maidservant’s questioning. This proves that no matter how great our works are—even enviable—they cannot last forever. Only the Lord’s lovingkindness upon us, with grace and truth, growing into the stature of Christ can do so. Everything else is pure vanity, chasing after wind. Because they can neither withstand the fiery trial nor endure.

玛拉基书 4章1节笔记
连恶人也是为刑罚的日子所预备的。虽然神的选民也曾经指着那些狂傲的人顶撞神说：“侍奉神是徒然的，这些行恶的人反倒得建立。”可是这一切，在神那大而可畏的审判之日，又怎能站立得住呢？
神的审判来得如此突然，以至于我们几乎觉得自己是在梦中。我们甚至觉得这一切似乎带着戏剧性的变化，而在此之前，我们几乎毫无觉察。难道不是吗？灾祸来临之前，岂是有明显的预告呢？亲人离世的突然，疾病的猛然临到，我们甚至感到无法相信。可是，谁能逃避呢？
**English Translation (Revised):**
Notes on Malachi 4:1
Even the wicked are prepared for the day of judgment. Although God’s elect once pointed at the arrogant and contended against God, saying, “It is useless to serve God; those who do evil are established instead.” Yet how can any of this stand on that great and dreadful day of the Lord’s judgment?
God’s judgment comes so suddenly that we almost feel as if we are in a dream. We even sense that everything seems to undergo a dramatic change, while beforehand we were almost completely unaware. Is it not so? Before calamity strikes, is there ever a clear warning? The sudden departure of loved ones, the swift arrival of disease—we can hardly believe it. Yet who can escape?

2但是，对你们敬畏我名的人，必有公义的太阳出现，其光线有医治的能力。你们必出来跳跃如圈里的牛犊。 3你们必践踏恶人；在我所定的日子，他们必成为你们脚掌下的灰尘。这是万军之耶和华说的。

基督是神本体的真像，是神荣耀所发出的光辉（希伯来书1:3）。祂能医好我们一切背道的病，使我们从死里复活，成为神的儿女，成为可被神悦纳的活祭。我们虽然被罪恶压伤，也必被基督医治得以完全。
**English revised version**：
Notes on Malachi 4\:2-3\:
Christ is the image of the invisible God and the radiance of God’s glory (Hebrews 1:3). He is able to heal all our backsliding diseases, raise us from death, make us children of God, and present us as living sacrifices acceptable to God. Though we are crushed by sin, we shall surely be healed completely by Christ.

4“你们当记念我仆人摩西的律法，就是我在何烈山为以色列众人所吩咐他的律例典章。

玛拉基书4:4的笔记：
“你们当记念我仆人摩西的律法，就是我在何烈山为以色列众人所吩咐的律例典章。”（玛拉基书4:4）
律法是为选民赐下，并不是为所有人。律法是那以后要来美事的影儿，同样，那美事——救恩，也不是为所有人，乃是为选民。当我们还在世上活着，我们的灵魂不能没有身体，但身体不能承受救恩。他并非没有用处，乃是像基督所说，神啊，你为我预备了身体，为要照你的旨意行。这身体要与基督同钉十字架，如希伯来书所说，要与基督一同出到营外被烧。而选民在世上也不能没有外邦人，但外邦人是在救恩上无份的。但同样，虽然我们的身体要与基督同钉十字架，但没有人不顾惜保养自己的身体。我们同样不能轻看、弃绝外邦人，如同昔日犹太人轻视外邦人。因为不是我们拣选了主，乃是主拣选了我们。我们并不是愿意或者乐意看到外邦人被弃绝，特别是那些逼迫教会的人，乃是愿意他们与我们一同悔改。这也如基督所说，人子来，是要救人的性命，不是灭人的性命。
**English revised version**（bilingual parallel for clarity）：
Notes on Malachi 4:4:
“Remember the law of my servant Moses,  the statutes and rules that I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel.” (Malachi 4:4)
The law was given for the elect, not for all. The law is a shadow of the good things to come; likewise, that good thing—salvation—is also not for all, but for the elect. While we are still living in this world, our soul cannot be without a body, yet the body cannot bear salvation. It is not useless; rather, as Christ said, “A body you have prepared for me, to do your will.” This body is to be crucified with Christ, as Hebrews says, to go outside the camp with him to be burned. The elect in the world cannot be without Gentiles, yet Gentiles have no share in salvation. Nevertheless, though our body is to be crucified with Christ, no one ever hates or neglects his own body. In the same way, we cannot despise or reject the Gentiles, as the Jews of old despised them. For it is not that we chose the Lord, but the Lord chose us. We are not willing or pleased to see Gentiles rejected—especially those who persecute the church—but rather we desire that they repent together with us. This is also as Christ said: “The Son of Man came not to destroy people’s lives but to save them.”

5「看哪，耶和华大而可畏之日未到以前，我必差遣先知以利亚到你们那里去。 6他必使父亲的心转向儿女，儿女的心转向父亲，免得我来咒诅遍地。」

玛拉基书4章5节的笔记。
没有一种咒诅比离开生命的源头更可怕。这是对我们而言。
对道成肉身的基督而言，没有比离开神更可怕的咒诅。因此，基督在十字架上喊出：“我的神，我的神，为什么离弃我？”
对基督而言，这便是最大的咒诅。我们并非说圣父离开了圣子——圣子不可能与圣父分离，因为神是三位一体的。即使在道成肉身期间，圣子与圣父也未曾分离，正如基督在世时所说：“从来没有人见过神，只有从天降下、仍旧在天上的子。”然而，就基督完全的人性而言，当祂被钉十字架时，神离弃了祂。若非如此，或若这种离弃仅是表演，基督就不会发出这样的呼喊。
当基督担当我们的罪时，祂是替罪的羔羊。神不可能既如往常般与祂同在，又将极大的愤怒倾倒在祂身上。当基督背负我们的过犯时，祂在地位上成为神的仇敌，尽管这并非因祂自身的罪。正如献祭的牛羊本身无罪，却因人的罪而被杀、被焚。神的仇敌必须承受神公义的审判和烈火的愤怒。而基督与我们最大的不同在于：即使在担当罪的时候，祂心中并未与神为敌。我们却既承担自己的罪，又心中与神为敌（雅各书4:4：“岂不知与世俗为友就是与神为敌吗？”）。因此，我们受刑罚是应当的。基督虽担当我们的过犯，却未像我们那样心中与神为敌。
对基督而言，背负人类的罪担、承受十字架的刑罚、面对人的讥讽攻击，都远不及祂所经历的至大痛苦——那位祂一向视为自己的一切、其旨意就是自己旨意、其本身就是自己一切意义与价值的神，在此刻竟视自己为仇敌并离弃自己。我们很容易被外在十字架的苦痛吸引，却忽略了那看不见的内在分离。亲密的朋友或夫妻暂时的别离已觉痛苦，若是永别呢？古人云“哀莫大于心死”，那种悲哀是外人无法看见的。
这也是耶稣在客西马尼园祷告时所说的“那杯”：“倘若可行，求你叫这杯离开我。”
当父亲的心离开儿女、儿女的心离开父亲时，神的咒诅便已形成，无法挽回。从亚当的日子到基督的日子，这咒诅从未消散，直到基督在十字架上完全承担了它，使神公义的心得以满足。
耶稣为施洗约翰作见证说：“你们若能领受，他就是那要来的以利亚。”是的，施洗约翰是基督的开路先锋，为基督作见证。若施洗约翰能成就救恩、使人心回转，又何需基督呢？这正如以利亚在迦密山所行的神迹：以利亚预备燔祭的牛犊、柴薪和浇在其上的水，正如施洗约翰为基督施洗。但行神迹的是神自己。正如以利亚所说：“使这民知道你耶和华是神，又知道是你叫这民的心回转。”那火何等猛烈，将燔祭、木柴、石头、尘土和沟里的水都烧干了。而主耶稣所承担的，正是为我们在神面前承受了这样的火。
神的咒诅并未消失，只是基督替选民承担了。因此，基督配得称为选民的主救主，配得坐在神宝座的右边，配得永远的荣耀、尊贵和美名，而祂的仇敌要永远作祂的脚凳。
**English version :**
Notes on Malachi 4:5.
No curse is more terrible than departing from the source of life. This is true for us.
For the incarnate Christ, nothing is more terrible than departing from God. Therefore, on the cross Christ cried out, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”
For Christ this was the greatest curse. We are not saying that the Father left the Son—the Son cannot be separated from the Father, for God is triune. Even during the incarnation the Son was never separated from the Father, just as Christ said while on earth, “No one has ever seen God; the only one who is from God, who is in the bosom of the Father, has made him known.” Yet, considered in His full humanity, when Christ was nailed to the cross God forsook Him. If this had not been the case, or if the forsakenness had been only performative, Christ would not have uttered those words.
When Christ bore our sins He was the substitute lamb. God could not both remain with Him as before and pour out great anger upon Him. While Christ carried our transgressions He stood positionally as God’s enemy, though not because of any sin of His own. Just as the sacrificial oxen and sheep were without sin yet were slain and burned because of human guilt, so God’s enemy must bear the righteous judgment and the fire of divine wrath. The greatest difference is that even while bearing our sin Christ did not harbor enmity against God in His heart. We, however, both bear our own guilt and are at enmity with God in our hearts (James 4:4: “Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God?”). Therefore our punishment is deserved. Christ bore our transgressions yet did not share our inward enmity against God.
For Christ, carrying the burden of human sin, enduring the physical torture of the cross, and facing human mockery were comparatively small matters beside the supreme agony of this: the One He had always regarded as His all, whose will was His own will, who was Himself the sum of all meaning and value, now regarded Him as an enemy and forsook Him. It is easy for us to be drawn to the visible physical suffering of the cross and overlook the invisible separation. Even temporary parting from an intimate friend or spouse brings pain; what of permanent separation? The ancients said, “There is no sorrow greater than the death of the heart,” and that sorrow is invisible to others.
This is also the “cup” Jesus prayed about in Gethsemane: “If it is possible, let this cup pass from me.”
When a father’s heart departs from his children and the children’s hearts depart from their father, God’s curse has taken form and cannot be reversed. From Adam’s day to Christ’s day the curse had not dissipated. Only when Christ fully bore it on the cross was God’s righteous justice satisfied.
Jesus bore witness concerning John the Baptist: “If you are willing to accept it, he is Elijah who is to come.” Yes, John was Christ’s forerunner and witness. If John could have accomplished salvation and turned hearts, what need would there have been for Christ? This parallels the miracle Elijah performed on Mount Carmel: Elijah prepared the burnt offering, the wood, and the water poured upon it, just as John baptized for Christ. But the One who performed the miracle was God Himself. As Elijah declared, it was “so that this people may know that you, O Lord, are God, and that you have turned their hearts back again.” The fire was so fierce that it consumed the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, the dust, and the water in the trench. What the Lord Jesus bore was precisely that same fire on our behalf before God.
God’s curse did not disappear; Christ bore it in place of the elect. Therefore Christ is worthy to be called the Lord and Savior of the elect, worthy to sit at the right hand of God’s throne, worthy of eternal glory, honor, and a name above every name, while His enemies will forever be made His footstool.

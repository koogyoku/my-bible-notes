# 诗篇 第2章

## 2:1-3

以神和祂的受膏者为捆绑与绳索的，这岂不正是那些被弃绝之人的光景吗？对于蒙拣选的人来说，他们却能赞美神的慈绳爱锁——祢的杖，祢的杆都安慰我。对于被弃绝的人，他却不可能真正知道自己的快乐其实是抵挡神的命令，正如保罗所描述的那种取死的身体。他自以为在追求美好，其实那不过是永远无法得到的海市蜃楼。因为若神不能满足人，就没有什么能真正满足人。那些所谓的“美好”背后，隐藏着仇敌清楚的目的——要与神同等。神岂不乐意向人启示自己，让人认识祂、得到祂、更多得到祂？然而人不但要得“饼”和“水”，还要用“偷”的方式去得——因为“偷来的水是甜的，暗吃的饼是美的”（箴言 9:17）。
**English version:**
Those who take God and His Anointed as bonds and cords—is this not precisely the condition of those who are rejected? For the elect, however, they can praise God’s cords of lovingkindness—Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me. For the one who is rejected, he cannot truly know that his pleasure is actually in opposition to God’s command, just as Paul describes that body of death. He supposes he is pursuing what is good, yet it is nothing more than a mirage that can never be attained. For if God cannot satisfy a person, then nothing can truly satisfy. Behind those so-called “good things” lies the enemy’s clear purpose—to be equal with God. Does not God willingly reveal Himself to people, that they may know Him, receive Him, and receive more of Him? Yet man not only wants “bread” and “water,” but insists on obtaining them by “stealing”—for “stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant” (Proverbs 9:17).

世上的君王和臣宰怀有自己崇高的理想——这些理想看似光辉且充满吸引力——因此他们将神的统治视为对自己的束缚。神成为他们唯一的拦阻。
正如始祖在伊甸园中自以为美好，却将神的命令视为拦阻。在始祖吃那不可吃的果子之前，已然将神的命令当作限制。他们固然拥有自由，但这自由被他们滥用了。
他们是否必然因神所赐的自由而犯罪？或者，若没有神特别的恩典保守，人就必定犯罪？
然而，神并不亏欠任何人任何恩典。恩典既是恩典，就不是债务。神拥有绝对的主权，自由选择将恩典赐给谁。
世人的代表——君王和臣宰——所谋算的无非是在地上建立天国。他们以爱、和平、自由、平等、民主、人权为旗号，或以共产主义、人间天堂为名。既然这些理想如此美好，可视为人类历史上最崇高的追求，那么任何反对、怀疑、拒绝或不配合的，都被视为敌人，应当以“人民公敌”的名义消灭。
因此，他们不需要基督——救世主。正如《国际歌》所唱：“从来就没有什么救世主，也不靠神仙皇帝。要创造人类的幸福，全靠我们自己！”
神并不反对人追求幸福、美好、公平、正义、自由、平等与人权。然而，在沙滩上如何建造房屋？人明明可以在磐石上建造。
人性中的自私、贪婪、嫉妒、骄傲与短视，谁能解决？此外，还有空中属灵气的恶魔，是人无法战胜的。
自二十世纪以来，人类已进行过大规模此类试验，均以失败告终。尽管哲学、科技、医学从未如此发达，却也从未有过如此大规模的死伤与残忍的杀戮和折磨。
即便人能在地上建立天国，人如何能永生而不犯罪？人如何面对神的审判——那赐下生命与一切生存所需的神，按其公义所要施行的审判？
人类不知道自己究竟需要什么，也不知道究竟在躲避什么。而他们所躲避的，正是他们所需要的——基督，神的受膏者。
**Revised English version**
Notes on Psalm 2:1-3
The kings and rulers of the earth possess their own lofty ideals—ideals that appear radiant and full of promise—and therefore regard God’s rule as a shackle upon themselves. God is their sole hindrance.
Just as the first ancestor in Eden considered himself wise and beautiful, yet treated God’s command as a restraint. Even before eating the forbidden fruit, he had already set himself against God’s command. They certainly possessed freedom, but this freedom was abused by them.
Must they necessarily sin because of the freedom God granted? Or, apart from God’s special preserving grace, is man bound to sin?
Yet God owes no one any grace. Since it is grace, it is not a debt owed. God possesses absolute sovereignty and freely chooses to whom He will grant grace.
What the representatives of mankind—the kings and rulers—plot is nothing other than establishing a kingdom of heaven on earth. They do so under the banners of love, peace, freedom, equality, democracy, and human rights, or under names such as communism and earthly paradise. If these ideals are so excellent, counting as the most noble aspirations in human history, then anyone who opposes, doubts, rejects, or fails to cooperate is regarded as an enemy, one that must be eliminated in the name of “enemy of the people.”
Hence, there is no need for Christ—the Savior. As the Internationale declares: “There is no supreme savior, neither god nor emperor. To create human happiness, we rely on ourselves alone!”
God does not oppose humanity’s pursuit of happiness, goodness, fairness, justice, freedom, equality, and human rights. But how does one build a house on sand? One can clearly build upon the rock.
Who can resolve the selfishness, greed, envy, pride, and shortsightedness inherent in human nature? Moreover, there are spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms that man cannot overcome.
Since the twentieth century, humanity has conducted large-scale experiments of this kind, all ending in failure. Although philosophy, technology, and medicine have never been so advanced, neither has there ever been such massive casualties and atrocities of killing and torture.
Even if humanity could establish a paradise on earth, how could people live forever without sinning? How could they face the judgment of God—the One who gives life and every necessary condition for existence—according to His righteousness?
Humanity does not know what it truly needs, nor what it is truly fleeing from. Yet what they flee from is precisely what they need—Christ, the Anointed One of God.

当我们做事时，往往从容易的地方下手，寻找简单的地方突破。这是我们堕落后的人性倾向。
然而神非人，祂的意念高过我们的意念，祂的道路高过我们的道路。我们不能像神那样行事，因为我们不是神。但我们可以求祂的恩典来引领我们。
祂的恩手乐意扶持我们，如同大鹰在小鹰以下展开翅膀，托住它们。祂也用另一种方式引领我们：从沙漠中开出江河，在旷野中开出道路；从磐石中吩咐水流出来，解我们的干渴。这些作为显明是神的工，而非出于人的筹算。
纵然我们经过千百次尝试，仍然难以完全明白，因为祂的作为与祂的救恩是神的奥秘。我们需要用永生去探究这奥秘。
**Revised English version**
When we act, we naturally tend to start from what is easy and look for simple points of breakthrough. This is the inclination of our fallen human nature.
Yet God is not man. His thoughts are higher than our thoughts, and His ways higher than our ways. We cannot act as God does, because we are not God. But we may ask for His grace to lead us.
His gracious hand is willing to support us, just as a great eagle spreads its wings beneath its young and bears them up. He also leads us in another manner: He brings rivers forth in the desert and opens paths in the wilderness; He commands water to flow from the rock to quench our thirst. These works make clear that they are God’s doing, not the result of human planning.
Even after countless attempts, we still find it difficult to fully understand, because His works and His salvation belong to the mystery of God. We need eternal life to explore this mystery.

信心是基督开给人的支票——因为基督是为我们信心创始成终的。神赐人这信心，使人能够把这张支票拿到神面前领受兑现。在这个过程中，神显明基督的应许是真的，神的爱是真的。神是信实的。神如此行，并不是需要人的配合、协同、协作、劳力，乃是让人在此中学习认识神。这是因着信，以至于信。不但信，也知道自己所信是真的。知道自己所信的是谁。又可以说，两个见证是真的。信心为神作见证，信心的果效——神的兑现，来同作见证。
**English version:**
Faith is the check that Christ issues to people—for Christ is the author and finisher of our faith. God gives this faith to people, enabling them to take the check before God and receive its realization. In this process God manifests that Christ’s promise is true and that God’s love is true. God is faithful. God acts in this way not because He needs any human cooperation, synergy, collaboration, or labor, but so that people may learn to know Him in it. This is from faith to faith. Not only do they believe, but they also come to know that what they believe is true and to know whom they have believed. It can also be said that the testimony of two is true: faith testifies for God, and the effect of faith—the realization by God—comes to testify together with it.

## 2:4-6

是的，“在耶和华的山上必有预备”。人的一切作为、功劳、努力、聪明和才干，都不能拯救自己，也不能带领人到神的面前。唯有神在自己的山上所预备的，才是照着祂的心意设立的君王。这君王虽为大，却要服侍小的，并献上自己作多人的赎价，从而领许多的儿子到神面前。
**English version:**
Yes, “on the mountain of the Lord it shall be provided.” All human works, merits, efforts, wisdom, and talents can neither save oneself nor lead anyone to God. Only what God Himself prepares on His own mountain is the King set according to His heart. Though this King is great, He serves the small and gives Himself as a ransom for many, thereby bringing many sons to God.

大祭司曾盘问耶稣：“你是那当称颂者的儿子基督不是？”耶稣回答：“我是。”
有谁能宣称“我是”？谁能如此说呢？没有人。
当约伯在苦难中，他开始咒诅自己的生日。尽管约伯完全认可“赏赐的是耶和华，收取的也是耶和华”，纵然他认为自己没有不义之处，可是眼前的患难依然使他恨恶自己的性命，以致否定了自己。在这种情况下，约伯或者我们还能仍然归荣耀于神吗？
因此，唯有神的存有才是真实的存有，是唯一的“是”。“我们纵然失信，他仍是可信的，因为他不能背乎自己。”
再者，儿子是承受产业的。谁能承受神的产业呢？唯有从父所生的子。这产业没有任何天使、人类或受造之物有资格或能力承受，只有子可以。因为“凡父所有的，都是我的”，“凡是我的都是你的，你的也是我的”。
此外，儿子是成全父旨意的。谁能明白父的旨意，谁能去成全呢？“你所托付我的事，我已成全了。”
我们不能说先有父后有子，因为子是父的道。有父就有道，我们不能说先有父后有道。尽管我们的思想在后天的发展过程中会发生许多变化，甚至走向对立，但我们的思想从一开始就与我们同在，连同变化本身也包含在内。
所以，我们的存有不同于神永恒的存有。我们是不断变化的存有，变化本身就是我们存有的属性。而神永不改变。如果会发生改变，那也就不是神了。
**英文翻译版**
The high priest once interrogated Jesus, "Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?" And Jesus answered, "I am."
Who can claim "I am"? Who can speak thus? No one.
When Job was in tribulation, he began to curse the day of his birth. Although Job fully acknowledged that "the Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away," and even though he considered himself free of unrighteousness, the present suffering still made him abhor his own life, leading to self-negation. Could Job, or could we, still glorify God under such circumstances?
Therefore, only God's existence is true existence; He is the sole "I AM." "If we are faithless, he remains faithful—for he cannot deny himself."
Furthermore, the son is the heir to the inheritance. Who can inherit God's estate? Only the Son begotten of the Father. Neither angels, nor humans, nor any created beings have the qualification or capacity to inherit it; only the Son can. For "all that the Father has is mine," and "all mine are yours, and yours are mine."
Additionally, the son is the one who accomplishes the Father's will. Who can understand the Father's will, and who can fulfill it? "I have accomplished the work that you gave me to do."
We cannot say that the Father existed before the Son, for the Son is the Word of the Father. Where there is the Father, there is the Word; we cannot say the Father preceded the Word. Although our thoughts undergo many changes during our subsequent development, sometimes even turning into their opposites, our thoughts have been with us from the very beginning, even encompassing the changes themselves.
Thus, our being is unlike God's eternal being. We are ever-changing beings, and mutability itself is an attribute of our being. God, however, is unchanging. If something is subject to change, it is not God.

## 2:8-9

祢求我的，也正是我愿意祢如此求的。
列国及万有都是藉着祢造的，也是为祢造的。
祢喜悦的是我的旨意，我的旨意是把我的所有都交由祢掌管；
因为祢是我生的，我喜悦祢，祢也是照着我的旨意管理这一切。
我的旨意是祢要去作多人的赎价，把许多的儿女带到我这里来。
祢不求自己的意思，只求我的旨意；
我的旨意是要把祢升为至高，在我的右边。
我要让祢的仇敌作祢的脚凳。
**英文翻译版**
What You ask of me is exactly what I desire You to ask.
The nations and all things were created through You and for You.
What You delight in is my will, and my will is to entrust all that I have to Your governance;
For You are begotten by me, I delight in You, and You govern all things according to my will.
My will is for You to become a ransom for many, bringing many children to me.
You do not seek Your own will, but only my will;
And my will is to exalt You to the highest place, at my right hand.
I will make Your enemies Your footstool.

## 2:10-12

诗篇第二篇10-12节呼唤君王和世上的审判官要省悟，受管教。他们当存畏惧侍奉耶和华，存战兢而快乐。
君王和审判官的权柄并非来自自己，乃是神所赐的（约翰福音19:11）。这权柄的本意，是要他们按照公义治理、施行审判，而不是用来显耀自己或牟取私利。然而，当掌权者不认识神时，他们只能按着世上的法律和自己的良心行事，因此世上常有不公义与冤屈不得伸张。
尽管如此，我们仍相信神全能的护理掌管一切。即使在许多我们无法理解的苦难、不公义，甚至死亡之中，神仍然有祂的美意。我们当将神的命令和训诲常摆在眼前，因为这是生命的条例。我们是罪人，仆人不能高于主人，学生不能高过先生。我们的主在世上尚且遭遇许多不公正的待遇，我们这些跟随祂的人又怎能期望完全不同呢？
因此，我们当求神赐给我们敬畏祂的心，使我们在战兢中却有说不出来的平安喜乐。这平安喜乐是神藉着基督所赐的。
「当以嘴亲子，恐怕他发怒，你们便在道中灭亡，因为他的怒气快要发作。凡投靠他的，都是有福的。」
我们当用嘴亲子，不是像犹大那样虚假，乃是像那犯罪的女人，用泪水连连亲吻基督的脚。这并不是说亲吻本身能使人得救，而是因着神的恩典，使我们认识自己的罪，并承认基督是唯一的救赎主。这才是人一生最终极的追求与意义所在。
然而，若没有神拣选的恩典，谁能找到祂呢？即使曾在祂面前听过道、奉祂的名行过异能，若没有祂的怜悯，也仍然于事无补。主啊，求祢怜悯，因为祢知道我一无所有，一无所能，一无所是。
### English revised version
Psalm 2:10-12 calls upon kings and rulers of the earth to be wise and receive instruction. They are to serve the Lord with fear and rejoice with trembling.
The authority of kings and judges does not originate from themselves but is given by God (John 19:11). This authority is intended for the purpose of governing with justice and rendering righteous judgment, not for self-exaltation or personal gain. However, when those in power do not know God, they can only govern according to human laws and their own conscience. As a result, injustice and unaddressed wrongs are common in the world.
Nevertheless, we believe that God’s almighty providence governs all things. Even in suffering, injustice, and death that we cannot fully understand, God still works according to His good purpose. We ought to keep His commandments and instruction before us continually, for they are the ordinances of life. As sinners, we should not expect to be treated better than our Master. If our Lord Himself faced much injustice in this world, how can His servants expect otherwise?
Therefore, we should ask God to grant us a heart that fears Him, so that we may serve Him with trembling yet possess an inexpressible peace and joy. This peace and joy come from God through Christ.
“Kiss the Son, lest He be angry, and you perish in the way, for His wrath is quickly kindled. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.”
We are to kiss the Son—not in the manner of Judas, but like the sinful woman who wept and repeatedly kissed the feet of Jesus. This is not to suggest that the act of kissing itself brings forgiveness, but that by God’s grace we come to recognize our sin and acknowledge Christ as the only Redeemer. This is the ultimate pursuit and meaning of human life.
Yet apart from God’s electing grace, who can find Him? Even those who have heard His word, eaten and drunk in His presence, or performed miracles in His name will have no benefit without His mercy. Lord, have mercy on me, for You know that I have nothing, can do nothing, and am nothing in myself.

# 诗篇 第4章

## 4:1

> 唯有基督显明神的义。神的义正是在此显明。
> 我们所披戴的是基督的义，是被神算为义的。

> 使我们真正困苦的根源，并非外在的缺乏，
> 也不是敌人或疾病，
> 而是离开生命源头的枯干。
> 那么，能使我们重新得力的，只有生命的本体——
> 天上来的粮，就是饼和水。
> 因为基督说：你们的祖宗在旷野吃过吗哪，还是死了。

> 唯有祢施恩的灵将祢的爱充满我，
> 我才能释怀一切的不如意与痛苦，
> 甚至能为仇敌祷告，
> 也不羡慕人间的一切。
> 甚至能把一切困苦都当作人间短暂旅行的体验，
> 如同人偶尔尝试苦涩或辣味的食物。
> 因为我知道，也许我很快就能到祢那里去，
> 这一切又算得了什么呢？
> 纵然长寿也不过几十年光阴——
> 人一生的年日是七十岁，若是强壮可以到八十岁。
> 这一切都依仗有祢的慈爱临到我。

> Only Christ manifests the righteousness of God. God’s righteousness is revealed precisely here.
> What we are clothed with is the righteousness of Christ—
> the righteousness that is reckoned to us by God.

> What truly distresses us is not outward lack,
> nor enemies,
> nor disease,
> but the dryness that comes from being cut off from the source of life.
> Therefore, the only thing that can restore our strength is the very substance of life itself—
> the bread and water that come down from heaven.
> For Christ said, “Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.”

> Only when You fill me with Your love by Your Spirit of grace
> can I release all dissatisfaction and pain,
> even pray for my enemies,
> and no longer envy anything in this world.
> I can even regard every trouble as part of this brief earthly journey—
> like occasionally tasting bitter or spicy food.
> For I know that I may soon be with You;
> what does any of this matter?
> Even a long life is only a few decades—
> the years of our life are seventy, or eighty if we are strong.
> All of this depends on Your love coming to me.

## 4:4

> 我们若知道神的公义，并知道我们犯罪是得罪了祂，
> 就知道所有罪的果效最终伤害的是我们自己。
> 我们是否如此恨恶自己的生命，以至于要自戕自害呢？

> 我向你犯罪，惟独得罪了你；在你眼前行了这恶，
> 以致你责备我的时候显为公义，判断我的时候显为清正。（诗篇51:4）

> 那时，我们必知道，神的律法是生命的条例，
> 是与我们自己的生命有益的。
> 偏离神的律法，就是偏离生命的道，必自取灭亡。
> 可是，我们凭自己无法遵守律法。
> （“摩西岂不是传律法给你们吗？你们却没有一个人守律法。为什么想要杀我呢？”
> 现在为什么试探神，要把我们祖宗和我们所不能负的轭放在门徒的颈项上呢？
> 我们的君王、首领、祭司、列祖都不遵守你的律法，不听从你的诫命和你警戒他们的话。）

> 正如罗马书所指出的，律法是让我们知罪。
> 不是我们应该靠自己遵行律法，而是律法指出我们的大过。
> 我们天天活在神的震怒之下，
> 既不认识神，也没有遵行神的律法，也不愿意遵行神的律法。
> 即便认识了这一切，即便地狱的火在我们眼前熊熊燃烧，
> 即便有人不断坠落其间，即便有人从地狱回来告诉我们那可怕的一切，
> 我们也没有能力遵守律法。
> 明明守住律法是与我们自己的生命有益，
> 所以我们需要救恩，不停需要救恩。
> 愿神施恩，我们好开始呼求。

> If we know God’s righteousness and understand that our sin is against Him,
> then we know that the consequences of all sin ultimately harm ourselves.
> Do we hate our own life so much that we would destroy it?

> Against You, You only, have I sinned and done what is evil in Your sight,
> so You are right when You speak and justified when You judge. (Psalm 51:4)

> Then we will know that God’s law is an ordinance for life
> and is beneficial to our own life.
> To turn away from God’s law is to turn away from the way of life and to bring destruction upon ourselves.
> Yet we are unable to keep the law by ourselves.
> (“Has not Moses given you the law? Yet none of you keeps the law. Why are you trying to kill Me?”
> Now then, why do you try to test God by putting on the necks of Gentiles a yoke that neither we nor our ancestors have been able to bear?
> Our kings, our leaders, our priests and our ancestors did not follow Your law; they did not pay attention to Your commands or the statutes You warned them to keep.)

> As Romans points out, the law is given to make us conscious of sin.
> It is not that we are to keep the law by our own strength, but that the law exposes our great transgression.
> Day after day we live under God’s wrath,
> neither knowing God nor obeying His law nor willing to obey it.
> Even if we knew all this, even if the fire of hell burned before our eyes,
> even if people kept falling into it and someone returned from hell to tell us of its terrors,
> we would still have no power to keep the law.
> Clearly, keeping the law is beneficial to our own life;
> therefore we need salvation — we need it without ceasing.
> May God grant us grace so that we may begin to call upon Him.

## 4:6

> 诗篇16:2记载：“我的心哪，你曾对耶和华说：‘你是我的主；我的好处不在你以外。’”

> 雅各书1:17也说：“各样美善的恩赐和各样全备的赏赐都是从上头来的，从众光之父那里降下来的；在他并没有改变，也没有转动的影儿。”

> 论到这从上头来的、从父那里降下来的，唯有基督。约翰福音8:23耶稣说：“你们是从下头来的，我是从上头来的；”约翰福音3:13又说：“除了从天降下、仍旧在天的人子，没有人升过天。”

> 那么，谁能指示我们什么好处？对于哪些问这个问题的人，正如耶稣为五千人行了五饼二鱼的神迹中得好处的哪些人，他们就说：“主啊，常将这粮赐给我们。”然而，祂的门徒中多有退去的，不再与祂同行。因为他们和我们从前一样，眼睛是瞎的。

> 而这岂不也是神的美意，因为祂叫瞎眼的能看见，能看见的反瞎了眼。正如经上所记：

> 「父啊，天地的主，我感谢你！因为你将这些事向聪明通达人就藏起来，向婴孩就显出来。父啊！是的，因为你的美意本是如此。一切所有的都是我父交付我的；除了父，没有人知道子是谁；除了子和子所愿意指示的，没有人知道父是谁。」耶稣转身对门徒说：「看见你们所看见的，那眼睛就有福了。我告诉你们，从前有许多先知和君王要看你们所看的，却没有看见，要听你们所听的，却没有听见。」（马太福音11:25-27）

> **English revised version:**

> Many people say, “Who can show us any good? Lord, lift up the light of your countenance upon us.” (Psalm 4:6)

> Psalm 16:2 records: “I said to the Lord, ‘You are my Lord; I have no good apart from you.’”

> James 1:17 also states: “Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights, who does not change like shifting shadows.”

> Regarding this one who comes from above, from the Father, it is Christ alone. In John 8:23 Jesus says, “You are from below; I am from above.” John 3:13 adds, “No one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven—the Son of Man.”

> So then, who can show us any good? To those people who ask that question, Just as these people were there when Jesus performed the miracle of the five loaves and two fish for the five thousand, at that time, they said, “Lord, always give us this bread.” However, many of His disciples turned back and no longer followed Him. For they were, like us before, blind in their eyes.

> And is this not also according to God’s good pleasure, because He enables the blind to see, and those who see to become blind? As it is written:

> “Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I thank you that you have hidden these things from the wise and learned, and revealed them to little children. Yes, Father, for this was your good pleasure. All things have been committed to me by my Father. No one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.” Then turning to the disciples, He said, “Blessed are the eyes that see what you see. For I tell you that many prophets and kings wanted to see what you see but did not see it, and to hear what you hear but did not hear it.” (Matthew 11:25-27)

> 这些发问的人，不过是又一次重演彼拉多对耶稣的质问：“真理是什么？”

> 他们分明是犬儒主义者和怀疑论者，擅长制造难题却无意寻求答案。他们以提问为专长，却轻视答案，因为他们在怀疑一切时自以为是，却从不怀疑自身以及自身的问题前提。

> 关键在于，我们每个人身上都投射着这样的影子。

> 这才是根本的问题所在。

> These questioners are merely echoing Pilate’s challenge to Jesus: “What is truth?”

> They are quintessential cynics and skeptics, skilled at raising objections yet uninterested in answers. They professionalize questioning while devaluing responses, arrogantly doubting all things except themselves and the very framework of their inquiries.

> The crucial point is that every one of us carries this same shadow within.

> That is the deeper issue.

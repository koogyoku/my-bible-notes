# 诗篇 第7章

## 7:1-2

> 我没有朋友，也没有可以依靠的，除了神。57
> 我不应当对人存奸诈的心，或者想要从人身上取利，即便是从自己的父母身上。
> 我当然应当存防备的心，因为我知道自己身处狼群之中。
> 但我不应当自己做狼，或者对任何人来说是狼。
> 所以，我不应当为利益跟人走到一起，
> 也不应当为利益活着。
> 若不是为基督的缘故，我不要和任何人走到一起。
> 在我还不能成为驯良的鸽子、或者像基督那样之前，即便我不能帮助人，起码我不会伤害人。
> 可是一旦我认识到四面楚歌，仇敌环伺，
> 我知道除了我的神，没有谁向我施怜悯，也没有谁的手能救我。
> 当我年老体衰、多病且意识模糊时，即便子女亲人视我为麻烦和重担，唯有我的神不会因我肮脏而嫌弃我。
> 既然如此，我一生之中，如果不以神为我的最爱且远超一切，只能表明我是被神弃绝的、不蒙怜恤的，即便我生活优渥，亲人自始至终不离不弃。
> 没有人能在我梦中也牵着我的手，教导我走义路、走正路。

> I have no friends, nor anyone to rely on, except God.
> I should not harbor guile toward others, nor seek to profit from them, not even from my own parents.
> Of course, I ought to be on guard, for I know I am in the midst of wolves.
> Yet I should not be a wolf myself, nor act as a wolf toward anyone.
> Therefore, I should not align with others for the sake of interest,
> nor should I live for interest.
> Unless it is for the sake of Christ, I do not want to align with anyone.
> Even if I cannot help others, at the very least I will not harm them, before I can become as harmless as a dove or be like Christ.
> But once I realize that I am surrounded by enemies and beset on all sides, I know that except for my God, no one shows me mercy, and no hand can save me.
> When I grow old and frail, beset with sickness and clouded in consciousness, even if my children and relatives regard me as a burden and a trouble, only my God will not despise me for being unclean.
> If this is so, if throughout my life I do not make God my supreme love, far exceeding all else, it can only indicate that I am rejected by God and have not received mercy—even if I live in affluence and my family remains devoted to the very end.
> No one can hold my hand even in my dreams, teaching me to walk the path of righteousness, the path of uprightness.

## 7:3-5

> 这样的人，亦即手里有罪孽、以恶报善的人，
> 必会被自己的仇敌追赶，
> 他的性命会被踏在地下，
> 他的荣耀会归于尘土，
> 无人记念，无人赞赏，无人敬仰，
> 甚至被历史遗弃，
> 唯独等待神末日的审判。

> Such a person—one with guilt in their hands, who repays good with evil—
> will be pursued by his own enemies;
> his life will be trampled to the ground,
> and his honor will be laid in the dust,
> unremembered, unpraised, and unrespected,
> even abandoned by history,
> waiting solely for God's final judgment.

## 7:6

> 时间不会因为我们焦急就变快，时间也不会因为我们愿意就变慢。
> 然而，我们终究有一天会发现并认同：
> 时光飞逝，如白驹过隙，
> 我们度尽的一生好像一声叹息，我们便如飞而去；
> 又像花草，早上发芽生长，晚上就被割下枯干。
> 我们不由得感叹，时间竟是如此迅速。
> 神的审判与救恩亦是如此——
> 并非祂耽延，乃是宽容；否则，我们之中有谁能得救呢？

> Time does not speed up because of our anxiety, nor does it slow down because of our wishes.
> Yet, we will eventually discover and realize:
> Time flies like a fleeting shadow,
> the years of our life come to an end like a sigh, and we fly away;
> we are like grass that sprouts and grows in the morning, but by evening is cut down and withered.
> We cannot help but marvel at how swiftly time indeed passes.
> So it is with God's judgment, and so it is with His salvation—
> it is not that He delays, but that He is patient; otherwise, who among us could be saved?

## 7:7

> 神的百姓尊崇神，因为真实经历了救恩。
> 神的百姓虽曾作过浪子，
> 但因着神的恩典，
> 得以回头，失而复得，死而复活。
> 至此，他们才真正明白：神是善的，神的爱是完全的，神是公义圣洁的。
> 神是应当被尊崇的，祂的百姓遂从心底发出赞美与欢呼。

> God's people revere Him because they have truly experienced His salvation.
> Though God's people once acted as prodigals,
> yet by God's grace,
> they have returned, being found after being lost, and brought to life from death.
> Only then do they truly understand: God is good, His love is perfect, and He is righteous and holy.
> God is worthy of reverence; thus, His people pour out praise and acclimation from the depths of their hearts.

## 7:8-11

> 看到世间不公义的事，大概没有人不痛心，我想即便那些因作恶犯罪而面临极刑的人，内心也是如此。
> 因为人若将不公义当作公义，看黑为白、看白为黑，并以此作奸犯科，那么他就应当坦然面对刑罚，甚至是极刑。
> 然而，我在神面前岂不正是如此？
> 因我心思行为的不公与不善，神的怒气似乎天天在我身上累积。但因着神的忍耐，我却误以为是神不计较，以致更加放胆犯罪，为自己积蓄了更多愤怒。
> 主啊，我在祢面前必然是如此。
> 倘若世人轻看祢、藐视祢，将祢的恩典归于假神、偶像或他们自己也就罢了；
> 可是，作为祢所深爱的我，也是用嘴唇亲近祢，心却远离祢。我轻看祢，藐视祢，将祢的恩典当作平常，将祢的宽容当作软弱可欺，以为祢必不察看。
> 我该怎样痛恨我现在的自己呢？

> Seeing the injustices of this world, there is probably no one who does not feel pain; I suppose even those who face the ultimate punishment for their crimes feel the same.
> For if a person treats injustice as justice, viewing black as white and white as black, and commits crimes on that basis, then he should calmly accept his punishment, even execution.
> Yet, am I not precisely like this before God?
> Because of the injustice and lack of goodness in my thoughts and deeds, God's wrath seems to accumulate upon me day by day. Yet, due to God's patience, I mistook it for His indifference, thereby sinning even more boldly and storing up more wrath for myself.
> Lord, this must be my state before You.
> It would be one thing if the world disregarded and despised You, attributing Your grace to false gods, idols, or themselves;
> but I, whom You love, also draw near to You with my lips while my heart is far away. I disregard You, despise You, treat Your grace as ordinary, and mistake Your patience for weakness, thinking You will not see.
> How I should loathe myself as I am now!

## 7:12-16

> 倘若我们所要受的苦楚是因为别人的过犯与罪恶，我们尚且有可说的、可抱怨的，甚至可憎恶和咒诅。然而，公义的神岂会行不公的事？祂终究要让我们这些恶人看到，我们所掉落的陷坑，竟是我们亲手为自己挖掘的。因此，当我们掉落其间时，便无话可说。我们发出的一切恶念与恶行，最终都像回旋镖一样，准确地射回自己身上。

> 反之，我们应当感谢神。若不是祂有慈爱为我们存留，并在起初拦阻我们过度行恶，我们今日又将面临何等灾祸。这真是一种矛盾至极的心情：我们既感沮丧万分，又因蒙恩而心存战兢——尽管这恩典不能减少我们内心的痛苦。

> 如此说来，我当为我今天不能如愿、不能随心的事情感恩。主啊，这是祢的爱。因为祢有丰盛的慈爱为我存留，使我们不至于顺着自己的恶心犯下大错、行大恶。祢拦阻了我，使我后来庆幸自己未曾随心所愿。

> 小时候，甚至直到如今，我常常感觉自己被各种外在条件所限制——贫穷、环境、能力……就像常常好奇山的那一边是什么。可是如今才发觉，那些不是限制，乃是保护。

> 动刀的必死在刀下。刀不是为了使用，乃是为了不用而预备。富有、条件、能力……拥有这些并非为了放任去使用，乃是为了在克制与不用中，使我们得以过平静安稳的日子，去思考人生的意义。

> If the suffering we endure were due to the transgressions and evils of others, we would have something to say, to complain about, or even to detest and curse. But how could a righteous God act unjustly? In the end, He will make us, the wicked, see that the pit we fall into is the very one we dug for ourselves. Therefore, when we fall into it, we are left speechless. Everything we launch forth behaves like a boomerang, striking back accurately upon ourselves.

> On the contrary, we ought to thank God. Had He not kept His steadfast love for us and restrained us from exceeding in our evil deeds at the start, what disaster we would face today. This is indeed a most paradoxical state of mind: we are utterly dejected, yet we feel a sense of trembling gratitude for being spared—even though this mercy does not diminish our inner pain.

> Thus, I give thanks for the things that do not go my way or follow my desires today. Lord, this is Your love. Because You have abundant steadfast love stored up for me, keeping us from committing great errors and major evils according to our own wicked hearts. You restrained me, making me later rejoice that I did not get what I wished for.

> In my childhood, and even up to now, I often felt restricted by various external conditions—poverty, environment, capacity... like always wondering what lay beyond the mountains. Yet only now do I realize that those were not restrictions, but protections.

> Those who draw the sword will die by the sword. The sword is possessed not to be used, but to be kept unused. Wealth, favorable conditions, capability... to possess them is not for free indulgence, but so that, in keeping them unused, we may lead a quiet and peaceful life, contemplating the meaning of existence.

## 7:17

> 我不是为别的活着，
> 我活着的意义在于照着神的公义称谢祂，
> 歌颂神至高者的名。
> 世间的事，眼看，看不饱；耳听，听不足。
> 不公不义的事自始至终都在发生，
> 可我知道，我的神在大水泛滥之时坐着为王。
> 世间的奸诈、邪恶与冤屈，祂都细察。
> 至于我，我若不在祂恩典慈爱的保守之中，
> 也必定陷在罪中，甚至是陷在更为隐蔽的罪——自以为义之中。

> I do not live for anything else;
> the meaning of my life lies in giving thanks to God according to His righteousness,
> and singing praise to the name of God, the Most High.
> The things of this world—the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.
> Injustice has existed from the beginning to the end,
> yet I know that my God sits enthroned over the flood.
> He closely scrutinizes the treachery, wickedness, and grievances of this world.
> As for me, if I am not preserved by His grace and steadfast love,
> I will surely remain in sin—even in that more hidden sin of self-righteousness.
